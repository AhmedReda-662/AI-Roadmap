## Design Context

### Users
A **self-taught learner, studying at night** on their own laptop. They're watching one more "how to learn AI" video after work, half-convinced this time it'll stick. The environment is dim, attention is finite, and the stakes are high (career, identity). The interface must feel like a trusted, calm mentor presenting a meticulous subject precisely — not a hype machine.

### Brand Personality
**Calm · Precise · Expert.** The voice of a careful, well-published technical monograph or a respected lab's lecture notes. Low noise, high trust, generous quiet space. Confidence without shouting. Serious instrument, never playful-fluffy or startup-screamy.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme:** Both, deliberately designed — default **dark** (learner at night), a deep warm ink (never pure black), with a crisp, fully-designed light mode as a peer, not a lazy inversion.
- **Feels like:** a precision instrument / editorial technical journal. Strong type hierarchy over dashboard grids.
- **Anti-references:** No purple→blue gradients. No cyan-on-dark glow. No generic SaaS dashboard. No emoji-as-icon. No glassmorphism. No repeating icon+heading+text card grids.
- **Signature motif to remember the app by:** "the roadmap as an instrument" — a persistent learning spine (like a subway line you're riding / a textbook running header) so the learner always knows where they are in the vast field.

### Design Principles
1. **Calm precision** — generous whitespace, restrained accents, nothing competes for attention; the content is the object.
2. **The learning spine is sacred** — progress and "where you are now" are always visible and never decorative.
3. **Editorial hierarchy over dashboard clutter** — fewer sizes, more contrast, strong type-led composition; kill grid-of-identical-cards.
4. **Warm dusk palette** — tinted neutrals (never pure black/white), one disciplined accent, deliberate companion light mode.
5. **Substance over ornament** — every visual element communicates state or reinforces the instrument feeling; nothing is decoration.
